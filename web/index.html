Felt & Chalk

8-ball on a 9-foot table or snooker on a 12-foot table. Sliding and rolling friction, side spin and draw, throw, cushion grip and squirt are all simulated.

Off: every shot goes exactly where you aim, with exactly the strength and spin you set. The bot is then close to flawless.
On: like a real player, your cue arm is never perfectly steady. Each shot drifts a little off your aim, comes out a little too hard or too soft, and the tip lands slightly off the spot you chose. The harder you hit, the bigger these slips get, so a smooth, medium-paced shot is the safe one and a slam is a gamble. The bot plays with exactly the same unsteady arm.

After a red you nominate the colour you are playing: right-click it on the table. Hitting another colour first, or potting a different one, is a foul. With "obvious colours" on, the game plays your shot 120 times in its head with three times your usual slips. If the cue ball always meets the same colour first, that colour is nominated for you. If not, you are asked which colour you mean. Your shot is then locked in as you set it and is played the moment you nominate, so nominate before you shoot if in doubt.
With a free ball the computer picks a ball for you in about 0.2 s and marks it with a gold ring. Right-click any other ball to nominate it instead. With this off you must right-click a ball before you can shoot.

Player profiles

A profile describes how your cue arm slips when human error is on. You set four things for each part of the stroke:

  • Gentle and Full power: the typical slip on a soft touch and on your hardest shot. Two shots out of three stay within this distance, the rest go further.
  • Curve: how the slip grows in between. At 1 it grows evenly with power. Above 1 you stay accurate until you really hit hard. Below 1 even a medium-paced shot costs you accuracy.
  • Tendency: a habit that pushes every shot the same way, such as always cutting a little to the right or underhitting.

slip = gentle + (full power − gentle) · scurve, where s is the cue-ball speed as a fraction of the hardest possible shot. The bot plays with the same profile you choose.

Built-in profiles can't be changed. Duplicate one to make your own.
